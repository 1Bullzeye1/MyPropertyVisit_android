# MyPropertyVisit privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Publisher: Mayuresh Sonawane, India

Privacy and support contact: [mypropertyvisit05@gmail.com](mailto:mypropertyvisit05@gmail.com)

## What the app does

MyPropertyVisit records property walkthroughs and photos, displays compass directions, organizes local property/visit records and lets you enter room details. This policy describes the current offline launch version. Live calls, cloud synchronization and online user accounts are not part of this version.

## Information processed on your device

The app can store property names, types and addresses/locations that you enter; photographs and silent videos; capture times, compass readings and visit associations; room names/categories, dimensions, units and notes; an optional profile name, email and role; and display preferences. Your profile email is not verified and does not create an online account.

These app records are processed in the device's local app storage. The reviewed launch application does not upload them to a MyPropertyVisit-operated server. It has no integrated advertising, third-party analytics or advertising tracking service. The reviewed compass implementation uses device heading sensors; it does not retrieve a GPS position or route. Addresses are entered manually. Media may itself show identifiable people, private interiors or addresses.

## Permissions

Camera access enables rear-camera photos and walkthrough recording. On iOS, add-to-Photos permission is requested when you choose to save media to Photos; that operation does not require browsing your existing Photos library. Android uses the operating system's media collection to save exports. The app does not request microphone or contacts access. Compass availability depends on the device and its sensor settings.

If you deny Camera access, capturing new media is unavailable. You can continue browsing existing records. If you deny iOS Photos saving access, you can still keep media in the app or use an available sharing destination. Permission changes are managed in device Settings.

## Sharing and copies outside the app

When you select Share, the operating system passes your selected files to the destination you choose. Saved/exported media can contain the visible compass and the property content shown by the camera. The recipient service's own privacy practices apply. We do not control or retrieve those copies. Saving to Photos/Gallery can place copies under your device's photo synchronization or backup settings.

MyPropertyVisit does not sell your app records or use them for advertising profiles. Device vendors and app stores separately process information for purchases, distribution, backups and platform diagnostics under their own policies.

## Retention and deletion

Local app records remain until removed through available app/device controls. You can delete room photos and property records and clear the optional local profile. Removing a property record does not necessarily remove related captures; clearing a profile does not clear property/media records. Dedicated video/session deletion controls are not present in the reviewed launch source.

On iOS, Delete App removes its local app container, while Offload App keeps app data. On Android, clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local app records; Android backup is disabled in the reviewed manifest. iOS device backups may include app data according to platform settings. A restored backup can bring back a prior copy. Photos/Gallery exports, recipient copies and backups must be managed separately.

The publisher has no MyPropertyVisit server copy to delete or restore. A media export does not include all editable property/room relationships and is not a complete project backup.

Send only information needed for the request; avoid private property footage unless it is essential.

## Website and hosting

The marketing/support website is separate from local app storage. Its hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver and secure the website. A private preview may require provider sign-in. The website does not add advertising or analytics scripts, contact forms or newsletter collection. Update this policy before adding services that change these practices. A support email opens your email service rather than an in-app upload endpoint.

## Security and use by children

We use the operating system's app storage and permission controls. Keep your device protected and review destinations before sharing. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed absolutely secure. MyPropertyVisit is intended for people documenting property visits and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly operate a service collecting children's personal information through the app.
